# POE2 Crafting & Trade Experience Survey

Help us understand how you tackle crafting and trading in Path of Exile 2!

## 📋 Take the Survey

**👉 [Fill out the survey](https://tally.so/r/VL4AEM)** (Takes about 3 minutes)

---

## 🎯 Purpose

This survey aims to collect player feedback on:
- Current crafting strategies and tools
- Trading behaviors and pain points
- Tool preferences and feature requests

Your input will help developers create better crafting tools and resources for the POE2 community.

## 📊 Survey Topics

### Part 1: Who You Are
- Playing duration
- Playstyle (SSF, Trader, Crafter, Flipper)
- Time spent on trading

### Part 2: How You Play & What Hurts
- Item evaluation approaches
- Crafting failure experiences
- Concerns when crafting

### Part 3: What You Need
- Feature requests for crafting tools
- Metrics you want to see
